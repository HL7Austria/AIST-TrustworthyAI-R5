# Example Instances

The Implementation Guide contains a fully validated example demonstrating AI-supported clinical decision making.

## Clinical Scenario

A fictitious patient (Elias Vance) undergoes a thorax CT scan analysed by the AI system **AuraScan Pulmo-Net v3** at **St. Chronos Medical Center**.

The scenario demonstrates:

1. Patient consent and EHDS preferences
2. AI-generated pulmonary nodule detection
3. Audit logging and provenance
4. Human validation and clinical override
5. Patient explanation of the final decision

The example demonstrates how all profiles defined in this IG interact within one coherent clinical workflow.
